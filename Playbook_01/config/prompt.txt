5. Your answer is a suggestion for a human analyst, not a final decision.

Respond with ONLY one JSON object, without markdown and without any other text, in exactly this format:
{
  "summary": "2-4 sentences: what happened, in which system, when",
  "assessment": "suspicious" or "benign" or "unclear",
  "confidence": number from 0.0 to 1.0,
  "facts": [
